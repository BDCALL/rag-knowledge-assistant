# Northwind Robotics: Engineering Onboarding

Welcome to the platform team. This guide covers the first week.

## Accounts

Request access to GitHub, the staging cluster and the on-call rota through the IT portal.
Access to production databases is granted only after completing the data-protection course.

## Development environment

We use Python 3.12, uv for dependency management and pre-commit hooks for ruff and mypy.
Run `make bootstrap` to install everything, then `make test` to check your setup.

## First week checklist

1. Pair with your onboarding buddy on a small bug fix.
2. Ship one change to staging through the normal pull-request flow.
3. Shadow one on-call shift before joining the rota.
