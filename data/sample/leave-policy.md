# Annual Leave Policy

Full-time employees receive 27 days of paid annual leave plus public holidays.
Part-time employees receive a pro-rata allowance based on contracted hours.

## Booking leave

Book leave in the HR system at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than three days.
Managers approve or decline requests within five working days.

## Carry-over

Up to five unused days may be carried over into the next leave year. Carried-over days
must be used by 31 March or they are lost.
